Routing strategies now draw routes: `route_bundle*` pass the matching `straight_metalN` cell (they previously fell back to an unregistered `straight` and placed nothing), new `straight_metal3/4/5` cells back the metal3-5 strategies, and `route_astar` converts DPorts to DBU, no longer passes the port positions as waypoints, and defaults to `metal1`. Route failures in `route_astar` now raise a `UserWarning` instead of printing.
